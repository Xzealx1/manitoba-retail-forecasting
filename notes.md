## Project: Manitoba Retail Demand Forecasting

### Problem Summary
Retail businesses in Manitoba often struggle with predicting demand for various product categories due to seasonal variation, promotional effects, and local economic factors. Accurate forecasting can significantly improve inventory planning, reduce waste, and optimize pricing decisions.

### Objective
Build a machine learning forecasting system that predicts future product demand using historical sales data, price changes, and seasonal trends. The model should produce:
- short-term forecasts (1–7 days)
- medium-term forecasts (1–4 weeks)
- long-term forecasts (1–3 months)

### What This Project Demonstrates
- Data cleaning & preprocessing  
- Feature engineering for time-series  
- Building baseline and advanced forecasting models  
- Model evaluation (RMSE, MAE, MAPE)  
- Dashboard/reporting for stakeholders
